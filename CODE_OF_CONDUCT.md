# Code of Conduct

`setup-birdcc` is maintained by the BIRD Chinese Community (BIRDCC). We want this repository to stay calm, technically rigorous, and welcoming for contributors, maintainers, and users.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific in technical discussions.
- Assume good intent first; challenge ideas and code, not people.
- Prefer reproducible bug reports, minimal examples, and actionable review feedback.
- Respect private infrastructure details, unpublished exploits, credentials, and other sensitive information.
- Accept review decisions and moderation actions professionally, even when you disagree.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, hate speech, discrimination, intimidation, stalking, or dogpiling.
- Personal attacks, insults, bad-faith arguments, or repeated hostile behavior.
- Publishing secrets, private topology details, internal logs, or unpatched exploit details without consent.
- Spam, impersonation, deliberate misinformation, or persistent off-topic disruption.
- Retaliation against people who report concerns or participate in an investigation.

## Scope

This policy applies to all repository spaces for `setup-birdcc`, including issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, release notes, and related community spaces where someone is representing the project or BIRDCC.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Project maintainers may edit, hide, remove, or lock content that violates this policy. Depending on severity and history, they may also issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban an account from project spaces.

## Reporting a concern

If you experience or witness a conduct problem, please avoid escalating it in public when that would increase harm.

Report concerns privately to the BIRDCC maintainers at `npm-dev@birdcc.link` with a subject like `[CoC][setup-birdcc] short summary`.

If possible, include:

- links to the discussion, issue, pull request, or comment;
- screenshots or logs that preserve context;
- when the incident happened;
- whether there is an immediate safety or privacy risk.

## Enforcement process

- We will review reports as confidentially as practical.
- We aim to acknowledge reports within 7 calendar days.
- We may ask follow-up questions if context is missing.
- We will not tolerate retaliation against reporters, witnesses, or moderators.

## Project standard

When in doubt, optimize for empathy, clarity, and contributor safety. A sharp review is fine; a sharp elbow is not.
